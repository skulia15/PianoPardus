# Starter pieces — sources and licences

Every bundled piece is a public-domain composition. The engravings come from the
[Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), which distributes freely
licensed typesettings. Mutopia publishes LilyPond sources, PDFs and MIDI; the
`.mxl` files here were produced from Mutopia's MIDI with webmscore (MuseScore's
engine) and compressed, so the notation is machine-engraved, not Mutopia's own.

Two of the eight carry conditions worth knowing: Canon in D is CC BY 3.0 and the
Chopin Nocturne is CC BY-SA 3.0, so both need attribution wherever they travel,
and a modified Nocturne engraving would have to stay under the same licence.
That is what this file is for. The other six are public domain outright.

**No in-copyright music is bundled, and none should be.** Popular film and pop
arrangements are almost all still in copyright: importing your own copy for your
own practice is one thing, shipping it inside a deployed app is another.
